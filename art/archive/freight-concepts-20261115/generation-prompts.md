# Freight sprite generation

Built-in ImageGen with transparent output. The existing Greyhaven IFV turntable was the style reference for both sheets.

## Locomotive

Use case: stylized-concept. Production-ready sprite sheet for a PC isometric RTS. The reference is the game's existing armored truck turntable: match its angular realistic pre-rendered vehicle finish, material detail and readable shapes. Use orthographic fixed top-down battle camera, 30 degree pitch, with diagonal ground axes at approximately 26.6 degrees from horizontal. Muted olive-gray and warm worn steel, beveled panels, dark recesses, visible industrial fasteners and localized grease and grime. Crisp edges, rendered realistic texture, restrained highlights. Paintable neutral light olive-gray panels, no text or logos. True transparent background, no grid or visible division marks, no rails or scenery or hard baked ground shadows. Crop nothing. Equal grid cells with generous padding on all sides. Each item in its allocated cell must be isolated and the same underlying vehicle from another yaw. One long low square industrial diesel freight locomotive, realistic slanted cab window frames, stepped steel nose, long vented dark hood, satin exhaust, heavy two-axle railway bogies, frontal yellow-and-black buffer beam, cream headlamp with no glow. Mid-body broad olive-gray side panel for faction paint. This is a locomotive, not a train carriage or wheels on tires. Strict 4 columns × 2 rows. The FRONT CAB and yellow striped front buffer point down, lower right, right, upper right across row 1, left to right. The FRONT CAB and yellow striped front buffer point up, upper left, left, lower left across row 2, left to right. Show the rear hood nearest camera when the front cab points away. Low compact industrial cab, formal scale of a rugged 13-meter locomotive. Keep complete balconies and couplers visible.

It is a symmetric freight wagon without a cab. Strict 4 columns x 3 rows. Maintain real-world width, same physical dimensions and pitch as the armored vehicle reference throughout. Consistently visible couplers and wheels, no extra objects outside the wagons. Use wide image framing so four columns and three rows can fit cleanly.
